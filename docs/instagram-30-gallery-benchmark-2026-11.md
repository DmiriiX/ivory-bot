# IVORY-ART — reference gallery benchmark (11 November 2026)

**Goal:** learn content presentation from 30 noteworthy art-gallery accounts without misrepresenting IVORY-ART as a physical exhibition venue. Its core product is original paintings and sculptures sold online.

**Evidence levels:** "High" indicates a direct public profile or published case study/website examples reviewed in this exercise. "Medium" indicates a reputable gallery known from the 30-account shortlist and a proposed *test hypothesis*, **not** a measured account-level Instagram content audit. Posting cadence, Reels ratios, highlights, or sales conversion are intentionally not asserted where we have no direct comparable data.

## Findings grounded in accessible examples

- Gagosian's 12 public posts shown for November 1–9, 2026 were 7 carousels and 5 Reels, approximately daily. **Source:** https://picmany.com/gagosian (snapshot dated Nov 10). Its highlights include Exhibitions, Quarterly, Shop and Videos. The model is *editorial depth* rather than merely high volume.
- Unit London developed using Instagram as a core discovery channel; verified by The Art Newspaper's 2024 case study: https://production.tan-mgmt.co.uk/2024/10/30/from-roving-gallery-to-londons-mayfair-units-social-media-journey-11-years-on
- Saatchi Art sells original artworks with curator-led selections, categories, prices and consultation: https://www.saatchiart.com/collections/all and https://www.saatchiart.com/artadvisory. The mechanics are curated discovery plus a low-friction next step, not copying their copy.
- Independent 2026 Buffer study: carousels yield higher engagement per reached user (median 6.9%) while Reels help discovery/reach. Source: https://buffer.com/resources/state-of-social-media-engagement-2026/
- Instagram-specific private stats for the other 27 accounts **could not be audited to a common 12-post comparable denominator** from public access. This matrix should not be read as rankings or proof that those accounts employ every proposed tactic.

## Current IVORY-ART baseline

- 30-day no-ad trial: Nov 9–Dec 8, 2026.
- Existing system: 4 feed posts/week (alternating 2 Reels + 2 carousels), Story sequences Mon/Wed/Fri, 18 prepared multi-photo artwork assets, English-first and Russian-second captions, tags in both languages.
- Three confirmed early feed posts; compare after 15 confirmed feed publications. Do not infer a shadowban from this small sample.
- Product destination: https://ivory-art.com/#ia-product-{ID} for current store overlay; never use obsolete /shop/tproduct links for Telegram/MAX. Instagram captions can't make arbitrary URLs reliably clickable; use site in profile and direct product links in channels that support them.

## Adaptation priority (no copying of gallery footage or artwork)

1. **Carousel / commerce:** Artwork fully visible on first slide (original aspect ratio), 2nd detail, 3rd another real original photograph, 4th confirmed scale / dimensions, final plain editorial buying prompt. Only use material actually available; do not invent interiors or artist statements.
2. **Reels / discovery:** 0–2 sec compelling genuine detail, 2–6 sec material/brushstroke, 6–10 sec entire artwork, 10–13 sec artist + title, 13–15 sec unobtrusive site CTA. Original licensed audio only. Keep art unaltered and do not fake painting process.
3. **Artist editorial:** Once per 4 feed posts include an artist-focused caption based on verified facts; default to name, technique, size rather than speculative biography.
4. **Stories:** Three verified frames: complete artwork, detail, truthful product title/material; CTA via profile link. If account supports link stickers in manual mobile Stories, use them; do not claim automated sticker support.
5. **Profile:** English-first concise bio, Russian line, explicit *online gallery*, original works, worldwide delivery when supportable, one verified link to modern catalog, pinned posts for best artwork / how to purchase / artists; highlights Works, Artists, How to Buy, Delivery, Reviews. Profile edits require Instagram account authorization and are not performed by this repository.

## Reporting and experiment logic

Every published post: format; artist; painting; scheduled/actual timestamp; link; reach; views; saves; shares; comments; reactions; profile actions where available; website clicks where available. Keep **unknown** separate from zero and aggregate only like-for-like reach denominators.

After 15 confirmed feed posts: compare carousel median saves / 100 reached accounts and Reel median reach / post; compare within-account early baseline, not Gagosian follower totals. Review sample-size limitations. Keep 4/week; don't delete followers or spend on ads yet.

## Editorial rules

- Title + named artist + medium + exact dimensions in EN then RU.
- Both English and Russian hashtags; rotate a modest small set of relevant tags based on verified media/subject. No fabricated genre or awards.
- CTA should invite viewing / inquiry without claiming limited availability or invented provenance.
- Premium brand: dark ink, ivory #f2e3bc, restrained effects; no watermarks or misleading mockups.
